COMPANY HR POLICY

Leave Policy:
Employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves per year.
Employees should apply for planned leave at least 3 working days in advance.
Emergency leave should be informed to the manager as soon as possible.

Work From Home Policy:
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Employees must remain available during normal working hours.

Working Hours:
Normal working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Employee Benefits:
Employees are eligible for health insurance and learning and development programs.

Leave Approval:
Planned leave must be approved by the employee's reporting manager.
